JavaFX Bank Simulation

Work Detail Page

Context / Problem

This project is a bank simulation that shows the imporatance of synchronization and multithreading concepts. The project simulates a bank with multiple tellers and customers. The customers will arrive at the bank and wait in line to be served by the tellers. The tellers will serve the customers and then leave the bank. The project also simulates the arrival of customers and the departure of tellers using multithreading concepts. This also shows that volatile data could be accessed by multiple threads and could easily be changed without proper synchronization, which could lead to unexpected results.

Reflection (What I’d Improve Next)

If I were to improve this project, I would focus on making the user able to do their own transactions as if they were a customer. This would make the simulation feel more realistic and engaging. I would also add more features such as the ability to choose different types of accounts, the ability to transfer money between accounts, and the ability to view account statements.
